BrainHelper

BrainHelper is a personal study workflow operated by its owner. It organises selected educational materials in a local Obsidian vault, keeps selected source documents in the owner's Google Drive, and imports selected materials into Google NotebookLM for study context. Generated notes and study plans are stored separately from owner-authored notes.

The desktop Drive connection requests the drive.file permission. This permits access to files created by the app or explicitly opened with the app; it does not request unrestricted access to the owner's entire Drive. Selected source documents can be copied to private, module-named folders. The informational website does not provide a sign-in form or access to those documents.

BrainHelper is for the owner's personal use. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, OpenAI, GitHub or any educational institution. Features are introduced in stages; this page does not promise an unattended service or availability.
