BrainHelper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scope and operator

BrainHelper is a personal study workflow operated by its owner. This policy describes the BrainHelper workflow and its public informational website. It does not replace the policies of Google, OpenAI, GitHub or the educational platforms used by the owner.

Information used

The workflow processes educational documents selected by the owner, module names, course identifiers, source versions and file hashes. It can maintain generated study notes, assignment planning information and operational status locally. Owner-authored notes are kept separate and are not modified by the machine workflow.

Google Drive access

The dedicated desktop OAuth connection requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It uses this permission to create and read app-managed folders and selected source files, upload source copies and verify transfers. The permission also allows management of files explicitly opened with the app. The current upload command does not delete remote files or change sharing permissions.

OAuth client credentials and authorization tokens are stored outside the Obsidian vault on the owner's PC. They are not included in this website or its source repository. Local credential storage must still be protected through the owner's Windows account and device security; rclone credential obscuring is not encryption.

Processing and recipients

Selected educational sources are uploaded to the owner's Google Drive and can be imported into Google NotebookLM. AI stages may use NotebookLM or Codex to process the task-relevant content supplied for that stage. These providers process data under their own applicable terms and privacy settings. BrainHelper does not sell personal information or send materials to advertising services.

Public website

The intended site is static and includes no BrainHelper analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, account registration or data collection forms. Its hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policy. Private notes and educational source documents are not published on this site.

Retention and control

Local source snapshots and generated versions are retained until the owner chooses to remove them. Drive files and NotebookLM sources remain in their respective services until the owner removes them. Stopping a local job does not automatically delete cloud copies. The owner can revoke the Drive app's access through Google Account third-party connections and manage stored data directly in Drive, NotebookLM and the local vault. Revoking Drive authorization does not automatically remove those copies or revoke separate NotebookLM access.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be dated on this page. Contact the operator through their public GitHub profile: Kinglam168 on GitHub. Do not post private documents or credentials in public messages.
